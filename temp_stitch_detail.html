Tanjung Buli Beach

location_on Maba District, East Halmahera

About this destination

Discover the hidden gem of East Halmahera at Tanjung Buli Beach. Known for its distinct crescent-shaped coastline and remarkably calm, crystal-clear waters, this destination offers a serene escape for those seeking to connect with the raw beauty of the Maluku Islands.

The beach is surrounded by ancient mangrove forests and vibrant coral reefs that are easily accessible from the shore. Whether you are looking for a peaceful afternoon under the shade of a palm tree or an adventurous snorkeling session, Tanjung Buli provides a premium experience in the heart of nature.
