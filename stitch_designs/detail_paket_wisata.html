Itinerary Timeline

1

Day 1: Arrival & Sultan's Welcome

Welcome to Labuha. Transfer to your luxury villa followed by a private audience at the Sultanate Palace of Bacan. Witness the traditional Ganda-Ganda dance over a welcome dinner.

2

Day 2: Widi Islands Hidden Gem

Full-day island hopping to the Widi Islands. Snorkel in crystal clear lagoons, enjoy a gourmet sand-bank picnic, and explore the untouched mangrove forests.

3

Day 3: Volcanic Treks & Hot Springs

A sunrise trek to Mount Sibela's foothills followed by a therapeutic soak in natural geothermal hot springs hidden within the rainforest.
