Festival of the Spice Islands 2024

Celebrate the rich maritime history and aromatic legacy of Halmahera Selatan in a week-long journey of art, cuisine, and tradition.

Experience the Land of Kings

calendar_today Oct 12 - 19, 2024
location_on Labuha Waterfront
group Open to Public
history_edu Annual Tradition

The Festival of the Spice Islands (Festival Pulau Rempah) is the premier cultural event of Halmahera Selatan. For centuries, this archipelago was the epicenter of the global spice trade. Today, we celebrate that legacy through a vibrant showcase of the Cakalang dance, traditional boat racing (Kora-Kora), and an immersive spice market that recreates the 16th-century atmosphere.
