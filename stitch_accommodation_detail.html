About this Sanctuary

Nestled on the edge of the pristine Maluku sea, The Royal Seafront Villa offers an unparalleled retreat into nature without sacrificing modern sophistication. Designed with the 'Halsea Harmony' philosophy, this villa integrates traditional Halmahera craftsmanship with world-class amenities. Wake up to the gentle sound of waves and spend your days exploring the nearby coral reefs or relaxing in your private infinity pool.
